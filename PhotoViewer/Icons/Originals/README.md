# 512×512 generated icon references

These PNGs are the 512×512 reference images produced by the `pixel-loom` skill.
They are kept for design review and provenance; the runtime menu icons are the
normalized 32×32 transparent PNGs in the parent `Icons` folder and are embedded
as WPF resources.

The two rotate icons were regenerated together:

- `rotate-left.png` is the model-generated canonical left-rotation reference.
- `rotate-right.png` is the separately generated right-rotation reference.
- The final 32×32 `..\rotate-right.png` is an exact horizontal mirror of the
  final 32×32 `..\rotate-left.png`, guaranteeing identical arc, arrowhead,
  frame, palette, and anti-aliasing at menu size.

Pixel-loom temporary download links expire 24 hours after generation; these
local files are the retained copies. Do not treat the temporary URLs as durable
assets.
